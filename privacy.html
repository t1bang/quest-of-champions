Play as a guest

New guest nicknames and game saves stay in this browser. You can play without an account or optional gameplay tracking. Game and hosting providers may process connection information needed to deliver the site. Embedded games and videos can have their own data practices.

Your Big Bang Arcade account

The first release of connected player features is for adults 18 and older. Players of all ages can use local guest play. We have not launched parent-managed child accounts. We do not ask children to create social accounts or submit personal information.

When you connect an account, Supabase handles sign-in. Your email is private. The arcade uses your chosen nickname, player ID, avatar, tokens, scores and optional cloud saves to provide account features. Your nickname can appear on leaderboards and in lobbies. Connecting an existing guest profile requires that browser’s guest key; matching names never grants access.

Optional gameplay activity

Activity sharing starts off. If you turn it on in your Big Bang Arcade account, we record site visits, features used (such as the library, leaderboards, lobbies and music area), games launched, approximate active play time and supported game events such as level starts, completions, deaths and equipment changes. Detailed progress events and cloud saves are currently connected for Quest of Champions and Blankman. All catalog games share the main-site account and can appear in cabinet and launch activity; games hosted elsewhere need a progress adapter for detailed tracking.

We do not send your email, passwords, typed text, exact movements or full controller inputs in gameplay events. We do not record your screen or use this activity for advertising. Only arcade owners can view the activity dashboard.

Turning sharing off deletes your stored gameplay activity. The daily retention job removes activity older than 90 days. Saved progress, scores and account records are separate and remain available.

Friends and shared devices

Private lobby codes expire after 24 hours. Each lobby holds up to eight players and shares only nicknames and the selected game. This release has no lobby chat or direct messages. Sharing a lobby does not make a single-player game multiplayer.

Use Sign Out when you finish on a shared device. It clears the arcade account session and local saves for connected games from that browser. You can download your arcade data from your Big Bang Arcade account. Use the support links in the arcade’s Credits panel for account-data requests.

Existing guest profiles

Older browser guest profiles may already have online tokens, scores or comments. Those records are preserved. You can explicitly connect that profile when setting up an adult account; otherwise it stays separate.
